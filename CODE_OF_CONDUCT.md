# DeepFurry Code of Conduct

[English](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) · [简体中文](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

## Our Commitment

DeepFurry is committed to providing an open, welcoming, and respectful environment for everyone.

We welcome people regardless of background, identity, experience, or skill level.

## Expected Behavior

We expect participants to:

- Be respectful and constructive
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction
- Give and receive feedback professionally
- Respect different perspectives and experiences
- Focus discussions on ideas, code, and problems rather than individuals

Disagreement and criticism are not harassment when expressed respectfully and constructively.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discrimination or hateful conduct
- Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual attention
- Threats or intimidation
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Deliberate disruption, trolling, or repeated bad-faith behavior
- Spam or abuse of project communication channels

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to DeepFurry repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, official community spaces, and situations where someone is representing DeepFurry.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to:

**faolanthewooolf@gmail.com**

Please do not use public GitHub issues to report conduct violations.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement

Depending on the severity and context, DeepFurry maintainers may:

- Issue a private warning
- Temporarily restrict participation
- Permanently remove someone from DeepFurry community spaces

Serious violations may result in immediate action without a prior warning.

Enforcement decisions should be based on documented behavior, not personal disagreement.

## Language

This document may be translated into other languages.

If there is any inconsistency between translations, the English version takes precedence.
